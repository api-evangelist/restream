But that&#x2019;s difficult when online postings often get hundreds of faceless applicants. If you want a chance to get called for an interview, then you need to leave a good impression on the employer.</p><p>One way to distinguish yourself from the other candidates is with a <a href="https://restream.io/learn/what-is/video-resume/"><u>video resume</u></a>. A video resume shows your personality, skills and experience all at once. Let&#x2019;s go over&#xa0; top tips for making a video resume, plus excellent examples of video resumes to help inspire you.</p><h2 id="why-would-you-need-a-video-resume">Why would you need a video resume?</h2><p>A video resume isn&#x2019;t just a work summary; it&#x2019;s a personal overview that&#x2019;s more insightful than any piece of paper. Making a video resume gives hiring managers a sense of what you&#x2019;re like the moment they see your name. This way, interviews aren&#x2019;t necessary for employers to get a feel for your personality; video resumes are memorable and insightful from the start.</p><p>In addition to distinguishing yourself from the hundreds of other applicants, you can use a video resume to show off performance-based skills. Acting, teaching, and presenting are all on display with a solid video resume. If you&#x2019;re applying for a client-facing role, such as a salesperson, a video resume can also show how well you do with people.</p><p>While a video resume can have a positive impact on your application process, a poorly done video can tank your chances of getting a callback. If you want your video resume to catch an employer&#x2019;s attention &#x2014; in a good way &#x2014; then you need to do it right.</p><h2 id="how-to-make-a-good-video-resume-9-tips">How to make a good video resume: 9 tips</h2><p>Want to make a video resume that employers will take note of? Follow these tips, and your video will shine rather than crash and burn.</p><h3 id="1-write-a-script">1. Write a script</h3><p>Creating the best paper resume takes time, and you won&#x2019;t finish your video resume in one take, either. But writing a <a href="https://restream.io/learn/what-is/video-script/" rel="noreferrer">script</a> makes the process much easier.</p><p>Start with your existing resume by putting its highlights in narrative form. Then, mention your relevant skills and why they&#x2019;d fit the position well. Bring up any previous experience where it could help.&#xa0;</p><p>Include hard numbers when you can &#x2014; maybe you oversaw a team of <strong>twenty</strong> people or contributed to a <strong>60% </strong>sales boost. Talk about your education if you think it&#x2019;s important as well. Finally, rehearse and fine-tune your script. When revising, the natural flow of your script should be a top priority.</p><p>Need help perfecting your script? Restream&#x2019;s free <a href="https://restream.io/tools/ai-youtube-script-generator"><u>AI script writing tool</u></a> provides you with a clean and captivating video script in seconds. Just type in your details and accomplishments, choose the tone you want, and hit <strong>Generate Script</strong>!&#xa0;</p><div class="kg-card kg-button-card kg-align-center"><a class="kg-btn kg-btn-accent" href="https://restream.io/tools/ai-youtube-script-generator">Generate script</a></div><h3 id="2-tell-your-story">2. Tell your story</h3><p>A video resume lets you do something a paper resume can&#x2019;t: tell a story. With a paper resume, a hiring manager has to piece together a whole person from text on a page. With a video resume, you can share something from your background that resonates with the employer.</p><p>Put your skills and experience into context by creating a story around them. Don&#x2019;t make a video resume just to rattle off a list of achievements.</p><h3 id="3-keep-it-brief">3. Keep it brief</h3><p>Video resumes shouldn&#x2019;t take much time to watch. You&#x2019;re presenting your candidacy for employment, not going after views on YouTube.&#xa0;</p><p><strong>60-90 seconds </strong>is the ideal length for a video resume, and two minutes is the maximum length. Cut down your time by filming a few script readings and removing the extraneous bits.</p><h3 id="4-provide-a-cta-call-to-action">4. Provide a CTA (Call to Action)</h3><p>The end of your video resume should urge employers to take some sort of action. How can they follow up with you? Do you have a portfolio website they can visit or a LinkedIn profile to look at?</p><p>If you post your video resume on a platform like YouTube instead of sending a video file directly, include relevant links in the video description.</p><h3 id="5-use-good-lighting">5. Use good lighting</h3><p>Quality lighting gives your video resume that sought-after professional look. You won&#x2019;t need a high-tech camera as long as you light your video properly. Don&#x2019;t stand under direct lighting, as it&#x2019;ll cast harsh shadows over your face. Use natural lighting when you can by placing the camera between you and the natural light source.</p><p>A good video lighting setup has a key light, fill light, and back light. Using all three lights balances contrast and shadows perfectly. You can read more about how to pull off excellent video lighting <a href="https://restream.io/blog/video-lighting/"><u>in our guide</u></a>.</p><h3 id="6-select-reliable-audio-and-video-sources">6. Select reliable audio and video sources</h3><p>For a higher-quality video, use an <a href="https://restream.io/blog/best-microphones-for-streaming/"><u>external mic</u></a> and camera. A standard <a href="https://restream.io/blog/how-to-use-webcam/"><u>webcam</u></a> and good lighting makes for a great video, but you can upgrade to a <a href="https://restream.io/blog/how-to-use-dslr-as-webcam/"><u>DSLR camera</u></a> to go the extra mile.</p><p>For a microphone, you can choose a standing/shotgun mic or a <a href="https://restream.io/blog/best-lavalier-microphones-for-streaming/"><u>lav mic</u></a>. Standing mics are typically either USB, which plug directly into your computer, or XLR, which requires an audio interface to use. While recording, make sure nothing appears in-picture to distract from you. With this in mind, using a clip-on or lav mic might be the best choice.</p><h3 id="7-boost-your-on-camera-confidence">7. Boost your on-camera confidence</h3><p>If you&#x2019;re not used to being on camera, take the time to rehearse and build confidence. Practice in front of a mirror, paying attention to your body language and expressions. Remember to keep your posture upright, relax your neck muscles, and hold your shoulders back.</p><p>On the day of the shoot, get a good night&#x2019;s sleep and drink plenty of water. When filming, look into the camera to simulate eye contact with the viewer. Wear something appropriate for an in-person interview as well.</p><h3 id="8-get-feedback">8. Get feedback</h3><p>After you&#x2019;ve recorded some takes for your resume, don&#x2019;t just review them on your own. Ask a&#xa0; friend, colleague, or mentor for constructive feedback on your best takes. A fresh perspective could spot something that&#x2019;s missing or overstated in your video. It may take more time to polish your video resume this way, but the fine-tuned results are worth it.</p><h3 id="9-choose-the-right-tools">9.
