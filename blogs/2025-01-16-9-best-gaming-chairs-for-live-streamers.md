Most chairs have many features and standards, but most of all, you must be comfortable sitting in it. Before you start shopping, consider your own preferences for chair features and customization that best fits your body type.</p><h3 id="chair-material">Chair material</h3><p>If you&#x2019;re investing in a gaming chair, it must be comfortable enough to sit on for a four-, five-, or six-hour streaming session. The chair&#x2019;s fabric &#x2014; leather, fake leather, vinyl, mesh &#x2014; will directly impact its comfort when you&#x2019;re using it.</p><p>Chair fabrics like vinyl and leather look nice and are easy to clean, but they&#x2019;ll get hot in the summer. An alternative is ultraleather or artificial leather, which is more breathable than real leather. Mesh and woven fiber chairs are super comfortable, but they&#x2019;re harder to remove stains from.</p><h3 id="mobility">Mobility</h3><p>For streamers on the move or multi-desk setups, chair mobility is an important factor. You&#x2019;ll need a chair with wheels to move around easily.</p><p>But some rolling chairs are better than others. You might prioritize quiet rolling so a&#xa0; noisy chair doesn&#x2019;t distract from yourstream. Maybe you want durable wheels because you move your chair around a lot (or you plan to use it for chair racing&#x2026;we won&#x2019;t judge). Check out the reviews before buying a chair to see what others say about its mobility.</p><h3 id="ergonomics">Ergonomics</h3><p>You&#x2019;re also going to need good back support for a chair you&#x2019;ll spend hours sitting in. Ergonomic design isn&#x2019;t just a fancy word manufacturers use to hike up a chair&#x2019;s price. A chair that doesn&#x2019;t put stress on your spine and promotes good posture is essential to your setup.</p><p>When <a href="https://www.spine-health.com/wellness/ergonomics/office-chair-choosing-right-ergonomic-office-chair"><u>choosing an ergonomic chair</u></a> for streaming, keep these features in mind:</p><ul><li><strong>Seat height: </strong>Look for an adjustable seat height. A range of 16-21 inches (40.6-53.3 cm) from the floor is ideal for most people.</li><li><strong>Seat size: </strong>From side to side, the chair seat should be between 17-20 inches (43.2-50.8 cm) wide. Make sure it&#x2019;s also deep enough that you have your back against the chair and about 2-4 inches between the front of the seat and the back of your knees.</li><li><strong>Lumbar support: </strong>Your chair should support your lower spine&#x2019;s inward curve. Without this, you&#x2019;ll slouch and strain your spine more.</li><li><strong>Back of the chair: </strong>The chair backrest should be 12-19 inches (30.5-48.3 cm) wide. Look for a backrest with an adjustable height and angle for a perfect fit.</li><li><strong>Armrests: </strong>A chair with adjustable armrests&#xa0; lets you rest your forearms and keep your shoulders relaxed. If your armrests are too high, it&#x2019;ll force your shoulders upward, which will make them sore.</li></ul><h3 id="style">Style</h3><p>Lastly, you&#x2019;re going to be on camera when you stream and viewers will likely see your chair.
