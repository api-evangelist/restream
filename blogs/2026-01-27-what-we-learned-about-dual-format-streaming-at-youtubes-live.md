<img alt="What we learned about dual format streaming at YouTube&apos;s LiveCon 2025" src="https://storage.ghost.io/c/90/06/900668bd-2aa2-42dc-9f32-86a461fa3125/content/images/2026/01/portrait---landscape.webp" /><p>YouTube invited Restream to speak at LiveCon 2025 in London, where our product marketing director Anya and senior product manager Matt <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0e3-EeGTyJo" rel="noreferrer">shared the stage</a> with YouTube&#x2019;s team to discuss the future of live streaming.</p><p>The key takeaway was clear: <strong>dual-format streaming is no longer optional - it&#x2019;s essential for reaching today&#x2019;s audiences.</strong></p><p>Here&apos;s what we learned directly from YouTube, what it means for creators, and how you can start dual format streaming today.</p><hr /><h2 id="stream-in-portrait-and-landscape">Stream in Portrait <em>and</em> Landscape</h2><p>YouTube made it clear at LiveCon that audience behavior has changed. Viewers now discover live content across multiple places - desktop, mobile, Shorts, recommendations, and notifications - often within the same day.</p><p>That&#x2019;s why YouTube emphasized the importance of streaming in <strong>both</strong> <strong>portrait and landscape at the same time:</strong></p><ul><li><strong>Horizontal streams dominate desktop and TV</strong> - your traditional long-form audience.</li><li><strong>Vertical streams unlock mobile discovery and the Shorts feed</strong> - your most engaged viewers.</li></ul><p>Creators who only stream in one format are cutting their potential audience in half. Dual-format streaming ensures your content looks native everywhere it appears - not awkwardly cropped or out of place.</p><p>Going dual format means designing for both experiences from the start. Your desktop viewers get the full horizontal layout they expect. Your mobile viewers get a vertical stream that feels like it was made for them&#x2014;because it was.</p><hr /><h2 id="the-numbers-behind-the-push">The numbers behind the push</h2><p>Over 30% of logged-in daily YouTube viewers watched live content in Q4 2025. In the US and UK combined, users consumed nearly 8 billion hours of live streams. Earnings from live automated ads grew over 130% year-over-year.</p><p>But here&apos;s what stood out most at LiveCon: creators consistently reported that vertical stream viewers are more active in chat and more likely to interact. Mobile viewers aren&apos;t just watching passively - they&apos;re commenting, reacting, and participating at higher rates.</p><p>Missing the vertical format means missing the viewers who actually engage.</p><hr /><h2 id="how-creators-benefit-from-dual-format-streaming">How creators benefit from dual-format streaming</h2><p>For creators, dual-format streaming isn&#x2019;t just a platform shift - it&#x2019;s an immediate growth opportunity.</p><p>Streaming in both portrait and landscape helps creators:</p><ul><li>Reach more viewers without choosing between formats</li><li>Engage mobile audiences who are more active in chat</li><li>Create better viewer experience on <a href="https://x.com/Restreamio/status/2014407968234557697?s=20">small mobile screens</a></li><li>Increase discoverability across Shorts and vertical live fee</li><li>Repurpose live moments into short-form content more naturally</li></ul><p>Instead of adapting content <em>after</em> a stream ends, creators can design for discovery from the start.</p><hr /><h2 id="restream-makes-dual-format-streaming-easy">Restream makes dual-format streaming easy</h2><p>While YouTube&#x2019;s native dual-format experience is still rolling out gradually, Restream already gives creators the ability to stream in <strong>both portrait and landscape simultaneously</strong> and available to all creators.</p><h3 id="stream-horizontally-and-vertically-on-the-same-youtube-channel">Stream horizontally and vertically on the same YouTube channel</h3><p>Set the stream orientation in your YouTube channel settings to Landscape + Portrait.
